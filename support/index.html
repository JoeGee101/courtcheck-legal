Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account or sign in?

Enter your mobile number, agree to receive a verification SMS, then tap Send verification code. Enter the six-digit code in the app. New users must confirm they’re 18 or older and choose an experience level. Contact email is optional.

What if my verification code doesn’t arrive?

Check that your number and country code are correct and that your phone can receive SMS. Use Change number to correct it, or Resend code once the countdown ends. If you still can’t sign in, email support.

How do I find a pickleball facility?

Browse Boards or Map and tap a facility for details. On the map, Use My Location can center the view near you. Tap Directions to park on the facility page to open directions in a maps app.

How do I check in, and why does CourtCheck need my location?

Open the facility page and tap Check in here while you are there. Allow CourtCheck to use your location while the app is in use. The app needs that location to verify you are inside the facility’s check-in area. If verification fails while you’re at the court, check Location Services and Precise Location for CourtCheck in device settings, then try again. You can still browse facilities without location access.

How do I check out, and why did my check-in end?

Open the facility where you’re checked in and tap Check out. A check-in expires automatically after 90 minutes and can end sooner if the facility is deactivated. CourtCheck does not use background tracking to check you out when you leave.

How should I read facility activity and status updates?

Activity counts reflect active CourtCheck check-ins and may be delayed. They don’t count everyone at a park or guarantee available courts. While checked in, you can submit a preset status for that facility. To report missing information or suggest a park, use Are we missing something? on a facility page.

How do I update my profile?

Open Profile, change your experience level or add, update, or clear your optional email, then tap Save Changes. CourtCheck assigns your username and uses your verified phone number for sign-in.

How do I permanently delete my account?

Open Profile, scroll to Delete Account, and confirm. Administrators can also use Delete Account on the administrator home screen. After a successful deletion you’ll be signed out. Read the Privacy Policy for details on records that may remain or email support if you cannot access your account.
